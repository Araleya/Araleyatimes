Data sources

araleyatimes.net is built on open data and could not exist without it.

This site is a personal project based on the open-source bustimes.org codebase by Josh Sherwin, with additional custom features and modifications.

Great Britain

Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0.

  • NPTG (National Public Transport Gazetteer) (places)
  • NaPTAN (National Public Transport Access Nodes) (bus stops)
  • NOC (National Operator Codes) (operators)
  • TNDS (Traveline National Dataset) (timetables)
  • BODS (Bus Open Data Service) (timetables, fares, and live vehicle tracking)
  • TfL (Transport for London) open data (live departure times)

Other

  • Postcode search powered by Postcodes.io
  • Map tiles from Stadia Maps

Vehicle information

Vehicle details such as liveries, registrations, and fleet numbers have been contributed by enthusiastic volunteers.
